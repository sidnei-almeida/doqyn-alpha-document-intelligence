## Tech Debt (accepted, non-blocking)

See `tech_debt` in frontmatter. Summary:
1. **Incremental translation scope** — Library/Viewer and some pre-existing Settings/HeaderUserMenu strings remain pt-BR hardcoded, matching the milestone's own "Out of Scope: 100% translation" decision.
2. **WhatsappInput (BR-only) still used in 4 non-signup flows** (share/signature/access-request/invite) — deliberately deferred.
3. **Cross-repo backend validation** — `doqyn-auth-service` may need to accept non-BR document/phone formats; client-side contract is ready, server-side (sibling repo) is out of this milestone's scope.
4. **Process gap acknowledged** — phase-level "no regressions" claims were asserted without a full baseline diff in 2 of 5 phases; both corrected post-hoc. Recommendation for future milestones: phase verification should diff the complete failing-test list against a captured Phase 1 baseline snapshot, not just spot-check.

**PASSED.** The milestone delivers a working, integrated foundation: automatic + manual locale selection with persistence, locale-aware formatting, and country-aware fiscal identifiers + phone entry for BR/PY/US, wired end-to-end from browser detection through to the signup forms and server-side normalization — with all issues found during the audit (2 real regressions, 1 latent Node.js compatibility bug, 1 incomplete translation seam) closed before completion, not merely logged as follow-ups.
